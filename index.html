More Than Metal

Every statue that leaves our workshop begins as raw sheet copper and a wooden mallet. Our artisans shape each panel by hand over weeks or months, chase the fine detail with steel punches, then fire-gild the surface in genuine gold before setting the crown and ornaments with coral, turquoise, and lapis.

It is slow work — the same work our grandfathers did, and their grandfathers before them, in the workshops of Patan, the valley the old chroniclers called the city of fine arts.

A statue is not finished when the metal cools. It is finished when it is ready to be worshipped.
